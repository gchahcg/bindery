### Added
- **Review duplicate titles for an author** (#1970): a new read only report, opened from the author page under **More, Review duplicates**, groups books whose titles look like the same work and says which rule matched: a punctuation or accent variant, a leading article, an edition marker such as Unabridged, or one title being the main title or subtitle of the other ("Mistborn" and "Mistborn: The Final Empire"). That last rule only counts at a colon, bracket or spaced dash, so a series opener like "Foundation" is not flagged against "Foundation and Empire", and it is skipped for two books that are known, different entries in the same series. Nothing changes automatically; excluding a row uses the existing exclude action, so nothing is ever deleted. Thanks gchahcg for building it.
